How we researched and ranked enterprise agent platforms

Last updated: 2026-11-09

This ranking is desk research. It is not hands-on testing. We did not run these products, and we make no performance claims of our own.

What we looked at

  • Official vendor sources: product pages, documentation, licensing guides, price lists and 2026 announcements from each vendor.
  • Public analyst material: ISG Software Research's AI Agents Buyers Guide 2026 summary, the public abstracts of Gartner's 2026 Magic Quadrants for AI Agent Management Platforms and for Enterprise AI Assistants, and IDC and Forrester blog research on agentic AI adoption.

Which platforms qualified

To be included, a product had to be a generally marketed platform for building, deploying or governing AI agents that take actions in enterprise systems. It also had to have current 2026 official documentation.

Ranking criteria

  1. Breadth of enterprise fit: how many large organizations can realistically adopt it, given the data and applications they already have.
  2. Lifecycle completeness: building, orchestration, tools and connectors, memory, evaluation and observability.
  3. Governance and security: identity, permissions, guardrails, audit, and the ability to govern agents from other vendors.
  4. Openness: support for multiple models and frameworks, MCP and A2A.
  5. Pricing transparency: published, understandable pricing ranks higher than quote-only.
  6. Independent recognition: placement in ISG's 2026 guide and inclusion in Gartner's 2026 evaluations. Inclusion is not presented as Leader status.

How our order relates to analyst rankings

ISG's 2026 AI Agents Buyers Guide evaluated 32 providers and ranked Oracle, Databricks and AWS first, second and third overall. It rated Microsoft, Salesforce, ServiceNow, Google Cloud and IBM, among others, as Exemplary. We rank Salesforce, Microsoft and Google higher than ISG does because we weight how widely each fits into existing enterprise applications, and how complete their builders are for business users. ISG's top three are strongest for Oracle application estates and developer- or data-led teams, and our entries note that. Gartner's public abstracts list which vendors were evaluated but not their quadrant positions, so we do not call any vendor a Gartner Leader.

Limitations

  • Vendor capability claims were not independently verified.
  • No public source gives vendor-level adoption share, so we make no market-share claims. IDC reports category-level adoption only: 50% of organizations deploy agents across multiple business areas, and another 27% in at least one area.
  • Prices change often and differ by contract and region.
  • Some 2026 features are in preview or beta.

Updates and corrections

We re-check vendor pages when pricing or packaging changes and update the Last updated date. Vendors cannot pay for placement.
